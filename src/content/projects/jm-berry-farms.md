JM Berry Farms is a family-owned strawberry grower in Victoria's Yarra Valley. They supply supermarkets, wholesale markets and food service, and hold HARPS and Freshcare certification.

Unlike a page-builder site, this one is written from scratch in Astro and uses three.js for 3D, deployed on Cloudflare.
